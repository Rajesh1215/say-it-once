Questions

Do I need to show my face?

No. Send a voice note or any recording without video and VoxReel builds the picture — graphics, images and B-roll — around what you said.

What can I upload?

A voice note, a selfie take, a podcast clip, or several takes or notes at once. Upright (9:16) or landscape (16:9) video, or audio alone.

Which languages does it caption?

English, Hindi and Hinglish are tested; every word is captioned on the moment you say it.

Can I tell it what I want?

Yes, in a sentence: who it's for, a number to make big, a logo to show. Leave it out and VoxReel decides.

How long can my recording be?

Up to about two minutes for a short. Longer recordings can be split into several shorts.

Is my clip published anywhere?

No. Your recording is only used to make your short, and never published without asking you.
